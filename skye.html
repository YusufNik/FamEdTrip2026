Skye Tour Options for Family

Trip planning summary for 7 people from Edinburgh – 1 September 2026, 3 days.

Best option for us: the Viator / Timberbush – Skye, the Highlands and Loch Ness 3-day Tour.

Reason: it appears to be the most convenient overall for a family group, with a smaller-group tour format, overnight stay in Portree on the Isle of Skye, and the possibility of arranging accommodation directly.

1st Choice

Viator / Timberbush – Skye, the Highlands and Loch Ness 3-day Tour

Viator page:
https://www.viator.com/tours/Edinburgh/Skye-the-Highlands-and-Loch-Ness-3-day-Tour/d739-6898P19

Direct Timberbush page:
https://www.timberbush-tours.co.uk/our-tours/from-edinburgh/3-day-tours/edinburgh-isle-skye-highlands-loch-ness

  • The Viator booking page appears to allow a maximum of 6 people in one booking.
  • It has an accommodation option for 1 September.
  • Overnight location: Portree (Isle of Skye).
  • Good option because it seems to be a smaller-group tour.
  • Accommodation can be arranged, and you wake up on Skye rather than off the island.
  • Some reviewers say it may be cheaper to book directly with the tour company instead of through Viator.
  • The direct Timberbush page appears to allow booking for 7 people.
  • It looks like the same itinerary / same Timberbush tour.
  • There are supposedly promo codes available for up-to 25% discounts
  • Timberbush can help arrange accommodation, or you can book your own and get a £10 discount.
2nd Choice

GetYourGuide – 3-Day Isle of Skye, Inverness and the Highlands Tour

https://www.getyourguide.com/edinburgh-l44/3-day-isle-of-skye-inverness-and-the-highlands-tour-t94986/

  • Appears to be available on 1 September 2026.
  • Can be booked for 7 people.
  • It seems you need to arrange your own 2-night accommodation.
    • That could be a significant hassle for a group of 7 people.
    • However, booking accommodation separately might save money.
  • Overnight location: Inverness (2.5 hours from Skye)
  • Some reviewers mention a lot of time on the bus.
    • It seems more focused on scenic stops / photo stops rather than deeper visits at each landmark.
3rd Choice

Viator – Skye Experience 3-Day Tour

https://www.viator.com/tours/Edinburgh/Skye-Experience-3-Day-Tour/d739-5256P19

  • Appears to be unavailable / sold out for 1 September.
  • But it seems available on 31 August and 3 September.
  • Overnight location: Inverness (2.5 hours from Skye)
  • This one may be less ideal for our family group because there seems to be a lot of driving / coach time.
  • Some reviewers say it can be better value to book your own B&B/accommodation rather than using the tour add-on.
  • Some reviewers also felt it was not worth the price.

Note: This summary is based on the available booking pages and reviewer comments, so details should still be checked before booking.
